PERSONALIZE YOUR SITE
1. Add your music file to assets/ and name it music.mp3. The top-right Play our song button controls playback. Music starts only after a visitor clicks, as required by browsers.
2. Replace the photo inside the rose: put your image in assets/ named photo.jpg, then in index.html change the image href from assets/photo-placeholder.svg to assets/photo.jpg.
3. Replace the six gallery placeholders with your couple pictures. Put images in assets/ named couple-1.jpg through couple-6.jpg, then change the six .svg paths to .jpg in js/script.js. Keep the six captions or edit them in the same memories list.
4. Click Let it bloom and wait for the slow bloom animation. The Open our memories button appears when the flowers finish opening; it reveals a six-photo carousel with arrows, dots, and mobile swipe.
5. Edit the initial love letter directly in index.html. Visitors can also use Edit this letter; their changes are saved locally in their own browser.
6. Open index.html in a browser (or use VS Code Live Server). Keep the css and js folders beside index.html. External Google Fonts require internet; the page has serif/sans-serif fallbacks.
