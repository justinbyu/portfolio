Services & Treatments

Each treatment area below explains what it involves, who it may suit, how to prepare, and the risks to consider. Suitability is only confirmed after an individual consultation.

Skin Consultation & Assessment

A one-to-one appointment to understand your concerns, examine your skin, and talk honestly about which options may or may not be suitable for you. You will receive a personalised plan, including costs and alternatives, with no obligation to proceed.

May be suitable if…

  • New patients unsure where to start
  • Anyone considering a clinic treatment for the first time
  • Patients wanting a review of their current skin routine

Before your visit

  • Arrive with a list of products and medications you currently use
  • Note any allergies or past reactions to treatments
  • Photos are only taken with your consent

Risks & important information

  • No treatment is performed at a consultation unless agreed and clinically appropriate
  • Recommendations depend on your individual assessment — outcomes can never be guaranteed
  • Full risks, expected outcomes, alternatives and costs are discussed at your consultation. Individual results vary.

Signature Facial Treatments

Facial treatments are adapted at each visit based on how your skin presents. Your practitioner will explain each step, the products used, and what to expect afterwards, including any temporary redness.

May be suitable if…

  • Dull, congested or dehydrated-feeling skin
  • Maintenance between other treatments
  • Patients preferring a gentle, non-invasive option

Before your visit

  • Avoid strong exfoliating products for 48 hours before
  • Tell us about any recent treatments or sunburn
  • Arrive makeup-free if possible

Risks & important information

  • Temporary redness or sensitivity can occur
  • Rarely, breakouts or irritation — aftercare advice is provided at your visit
  • Full risks, expected outcomes, alternatives and costs are discussed at your consultation. Individual results vary.

Skin Rejuvenation

Rejuvenation plans are built gradually. After assessment, your practitioner explains the proposed treatment type, expected downtime, number of sessions typically involved, and the realistic range of outcomes before you decide.

May be suitable if…

  • Uneven tone or texture concerns
  • Fine lines and tired-looking skin
  • Patients able to follow a structured aftercare routine

Before your visit

  • Sun protection and product-use instructions are given in advance
  • Some treatments require pausing active skincare — you will be advised
  • Plan around important events where downtime may apply

Risks & important information

  • Redness, peeling or sensitivity depending on treatment depth
  • Pigment changes are possible, particularly with sun exposure — strict SPF aftercare matters
  • Full risks, expected outcomes, alternatives and costs are discussed at your consultation. Individual results vary.

Injectables Consultation

Some injectable treatments involve prescription-only medicines and are discussed and provided only after a full medical assessment, in line with applicable regulations. Your consultation covers expected effects, longevity, risks, costs and non-injectable alternatives, so you can decide without pressure.

May be suitable if…

  • Adults who have completed a medical assessment
  • Patients seeking a natural, measured approach
  • Those wanting risks and alternatives explained first

Before your visit

  • Bring details of medications, allergies and previous treatments
  • Avoid booking immediately before major events
  • You will be asked about pregnancy, breastfeeding and relevant medical history

Risks & important information

  • Bruising, swelling, asymmetry or tenderness can occur
  • Less common risks — including vascular events with fillers — are explained in full during consultation
  • Full risks, expected outcomes, alternatives and costs are discussed at your consultation. Individual results vary.

Laser & Light Treatments

Laser and light technologies differ by concern and skin type. Your practitioner will confirm whether a device treatment is appropriate for you, arrange a patch test where required, and explain session spacing, sensations during treatment and aftercare.

May be suitable if…

  • Concerns confirmed suitable for device treatment at assessment
  • Patients who can avoid tanning before and after sessions
  • Those able to attend a course of spaced appointments

Before your visit

  • Avoid sun exposure and fake tan as advised before treatment
  • Tell us about recent medications that increase light sensitivity
  • A patch test may be required before a full session

Risks & important information

  • Temporary redness, warmth or swelling after treatment
  • Pigment changes or blistering are uncommon but possible — aftercare reduces risk
  • Full risks, expected outcomes, alternatives and costs are discussed at your consultation. Individual results vary.

Body Contouring Consultation

Non-surgical body treatments are not weight-loss treatments and are not suitable for everyone. Your consultation covers how the technology works, the areas it can address, the number of sessions typically involved, and lifestyle factors that influence results.

May be suitable if…

  • Adults at a stable weight seeking targeted treatment areas
  • Patients with realistic, gradual expectations
  • Those assessed as suitable after medical screening

Before your visit

  • You will be asked about relevant medical history and implants
  • Wear comfortable clothing for assessment
  • Measurements or photos only with your consent

Risks & important information

  • Temporary numbness, redness, bruising or swelling can occur
  • Results vary between individuals and cannot be guaranteed
  • Full risks, expected outcomes, alternatives and costs are discussed at your consultation. Individual results vary.
