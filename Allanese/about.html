About

About the Clinic

Allanese Medical Aesthetics was created around a simple belief: aesthetic care should begin with listening — and every recommendation should be explainable.

Our story

A calmer, more considered clinic experience

Our approach pairs clinical care with a serene, private setting. Appointments are unhurried, questions are welcome, and "not yet" — or "not suitable" — is always an acceptable answer from us.

We build plans gradually and review them with you, rather than promising dramatic change. If a treatment isn't right for your skin, your health, or your goals, we will explain why and discuss alternatives — including doing nothing at all.

What guides us

Our care principles

Consultation-first

Every plan starts with a private assessment of your skin, goals and suitability — before any treatment is recommended.

Personalised plans

Recommendations, session counts and costs are tailored to you and explained in writing at your consultation.

Risks, honestly explained

Benefits, limitations, downtime and risks — including uncommon ones — are discussed before you consent to anything.

Privacy respected

We collect only the details needed to respond to your request. No medical details are asked for through this website's form.
