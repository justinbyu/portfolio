Privacy Policy

What personal information this website collects, why, who can access it, and how to contact us about it.

Template notice: this policy is a starting template for the prototype and must be replaced with clinic-approved wording before launch. The prototype appointment form currently does not transmit or store any information.

1. Who we are

Allanese Medical Aesthetics ("the clinic", "we", "us"). Official clinic name, address and privacy contact: To confirm

2. What we collect through this website

When the booking workflow is live, the appointment request form will collect only: your name, contact number, optional email, the service you're interested in, your preferred date/time, and an optional short message. The form is not for medical information — please never send diagnoses, medical histories, prescriptions or photos through it.

3. Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your appointment request, contact you to confirm or rearrange an appointment, and keep the minimal records needed to manage bookings. We do not sell personal information.

4. Who can access it

Only authorised clinic staff who need it to manage your booking. If a third-party booking or email provider is used, it will be named here, together with how it processes data on our behalf. Provider to confirm

5. How long we keep it

Appointment enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage bookings, then deleted or anonymised. Clinical records, if you become a patient, are held separately under the clinic's clinical record-keeping and retention policies — not through this website. Retention periods to confirm

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for deletion where applicable law allows. Contact the clinic's privacy contact to make a request. Privacy contact to confirm

7. Cookies and analytics

This prototype does not use tracking cookies. If analytics or embedded maps/social feeds are added at launch, the cookies they set will be listed here, with consent controls where required.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with its effective date. Effective date: To confirm
