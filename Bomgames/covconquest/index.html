How a battle is fought

The Land

Three zones — Zarahemla, Jershon, and Bountiful. Hold a zone with a Fortification, or by fielding two or more units in it. Destroy everything in a zone and it goes neutral.

Resources

You gain +2 of your resource each turn — +3 from round 6 on (cap 12). Nephites spend Faith, Lamanites spend Might. You draw 1 card per turn, up to 7 in hand.

Combat

Click one of your units, then click a zone to attack it. Fortifications block a zone, and their shield is consumed as it absorbs damage. If the attacker survives and no defenders remain, the zone changes hands. Each unit attacks once per round.

Winning

Lamanites: hold all 3 zones, or drain enemy Faith to 0 while their High Priests are gone. Nephites: survive all 10 rounds holding a fortified city, or destroy 3 enemy leaders.
